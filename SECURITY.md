# Security policy

This service holds the Google identity behind every Syllabus account, the
bearer tokens that let a Mac prove which account it belongs to, the Drive grant
those Macs upload with, and the relay that carries a browser through to a
panel. It is on the public internet at `syllabusaccounts.maincoursemedia.com`.
Reports are genuinely welcome.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private reporting:
[open an advisory](https://github.com/trace-J/syllabus-accounts/security/advisories/new)
and describe what you found. The report stays between us until there is a fix.
Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a security problem.

This is a one-person project with no bug bounty and no paid on-call. Expect a
first reply within about a week. If you believe something is being exploited
right now, say so in the title and it moves to the front.

A useful report names the endpoint, what an attacker ends up able to read or
do, and the shortest request sequence that demonstrates it.

## Testing against the live service

Only against your own account and your own devices. Do not attempt to reach
another account's session, device token, Drive grant, or panel, and do not
load test, flood, or otherwise try to take the service down. If a proof
needs a second account, make one; if it cannot be shown without touching
someone else's, describe it instead and we will reproduce it here.

## Versions that get fixes

Whatever is deployed, which tracks `main`. There are no released versions to
backport to.

## In scope

- Google sign-in and the session cookie: how it is signed, scoped, and
  expired.
- Device claim: the code exchange, the bearer token, and anything that lets
  one device act as another.
- The panel relay, including whether a request can reach a panel belonging to
  an account that is not the requester's.
- The stored Google Drive grant and the refresh token behind it.
- Schedule sync and any other account data in D1, including cross-account
  reads and injection into queries.

## Out of scope

- **The Syllabus app itself.** Recording, transcription, the summary, the
  local panel, and the packaged Mac app live in
  [LectureAI](https://github.com/trace-J/LectureAI) and have their own policy.
- **Bugs in Cloudflare, D1, or Google**, which go upstream.
- Reports generated by a scanner with no demonstrated impact: a missing header
  on an endpoint that serves no content, a cookie flag that changes nothing
  given how the cookie is used, or a version banner.
- Anything that assumes the attacker already has the victim's Google account.
